# Content sources

The descriptions in this independent atlas are short, original summaries. These official resources were consulted on 11 October 2026. Visitor prices and opening times are intentionally omitted because they change.

| Landmark | Official resource | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Big Ben | [UK Parliament — Big Ben](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/big-ben/) | Bell, clock tower and Westminster setting |
| Tower Bridge | [Tower Bridge — History](https://www.towerbridge.org.uk/stories/history) | Opening in 1894, architecture and lifting bridge |
| British Museum | [British Museum — The British Museum story](https://www.britishmuseum.org/about-us/british-museum-story) | Foundation in 1753 and opening in 1759 |
| Buckingham Palace | [Royal Collection Trust — Who lived at Buckingham Palace?](https://www.rct.uk/visit/buckingham-palace/who-lived-at-buckingham-palace) | Royal court and Queen Victoria in 1837 |
| London Eye | [London Eye — FAQs](https://www.londoneye.com/plan-your-visit/information-and-support/faqs/) | Height of 135 metres and South Bank setting |
| Westminster Abbey | [Westminster Abbey — A history of coronations](https://www.westminster-abbey.org/history/coronations-at-the-abbey/a-history-of-coronations) | Coronation church since 1066 |

Map positions and surrounding architecture are stylised artistic interpretations, rather than surveyed geography or walking directions. The six landmark illustrations and atmospheric backdrop were generated for this project; no third-party tourist photographs are embedded.
